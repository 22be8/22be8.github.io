Health Check

Calculate Your BMI

Body Mass Index (BMI) compares your weight to your height. It's a quick screening tool that can be a helpful starting point when setting fitness goals.

  • UnderweightBelow 18.5
  • Healthy weight18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight25 – 29.9
  • Obese30 and above

BMI doesn't distinguish between muscle and fat and isn't designed for children, pregnant women or very muscular people. Speak to a healthcare professional for a full assessment.

Fitness Tips

Small Habits, Big Results

Simple, practical advice to help you train safely, recover well and stay consistent.

Basics

Always Warm Up First

Spend 5–10 minutes on light cardio and dynamic movements like leg swings and arm circles. A good warm-up raises body temperature and prepares your joints for harder work.

⏱ 2 min read
Nutrition

Fuel Your Workouts

Include a source of protein with each meal, eat plenty of vegetables and whole foods, and drink water throughout the day, especially before and after you train.

⏱ 3 min read
Recovery

Rest Is Part of Training

Muscles get stronger while you recover. Aim for 7–9 hours of sleep, take at least one rest day a week, and increase weights or distance gradually.

⏱ 2 min read
