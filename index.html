Travel Tips

Smart Tips for Better Trips

Simple advice from our travel experts to help you save money, stay safe and travel well.

Budget

Book Flights at the Right Time

Booking international flights a few months ahead and staying flexible by a day or two can noticeably lower fares. Set price alerts and compare nearby airports.

⏱ 2 min read
Planning

Pack Light, Travel Smart

Stick to versatile clothing, roll instead of fold, and keep essentials, medicines and copies of documents in your carry-on in case checked bags are delayed.

⏱ 3 min read
Culture

Respect Local Customs

Learn a few words of the local language, check dress codes for religious sites and read up on tipping etiquette. Small gestures make a big difference.

⏱ 2 min read
