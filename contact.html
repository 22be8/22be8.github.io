Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us a message above.

I'm a complete beginner. Where should I start?

Start with the Full-Body Strength or Couch to 5K program and the Beginner weekly plan. Both begin gently and build up gradually, so you can learn good technique before adding intensity.

Do I need gym equipment?

No. Most workouts include bodyweight alternatives you can do at home. A pair of dumbbells or resistance bands can add variety, but they aren't required to get started.

How often should I work out?

For general health, aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week plus muscle-strengthening exercise on two or more days. Our weekly plans are built around this, with rest days included.

Do you offer nutrition guidance?

We share general healthy-eating tips to support your training. For a personalised diet or a medical condition, please speak to a registered dietitian or doctor.

Is the fitness consultation free?

Yes. Your first consultation and program recommendation are completely free, with no obligation to sign up.
